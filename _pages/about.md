Welcome! I am an associate professor with the [Department of Signal Processing and Communications](https://www.tsc.uc3m.es) at the [Universidad Carlos III de Madrid](https://www.uc3m.es)

Brief CV
======
I've got a PhD in Communications Technologies from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Spain, in 2003, and a Degree in Telecommunications Engineering from Universidad de Valladolid, Spain, in 1999.

I have spent most of my professional life at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, but I have also worked at the University of Southampton as a Research Fellow (2004-2005) and at BBVA as a Senior Machine Learning Expert (2014-2019).


Research Interests
======

My main research topic is Machine Learning, mostly **Kernel Methods** (Support Vector Machines, Spectral Clustering...) and their application in health and finances.


Teaching @ UC3M
======

This course I'm teaching Modern Theory of Detection and Estimation to 3rd year undergrads in Telecommunications Engineering, Machine Learning 2 to 3rd year undergrads in Data Science and Engineering and Machine Learning in the Masters in Big Data Analytics.
